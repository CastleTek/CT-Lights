ESP-NOW


Choose Wi-Fi / LAN or CT-SYNC after the controller settings load.
Controllers sync only through the selected transport. They do not automatically switch or fall back to another transport.

The radio is required for CT-SYNC and compatible remotes. Wi-Fi / LAN sync keeps it off.

After a failed Wi-Fi connection attempt, the controller stays on this offline channel until you reconnect Wi-Fi, restart, save network settings, or select Wi-Fi / LAN sync. Scanning for Wi-Fi networks briefly interrupts CT-SYNC.
